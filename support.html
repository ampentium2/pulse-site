Support

Pulse is a one-person project. The fastest way to get a useful reply is from inside the app — the steps below explain why, and an email fallback is at the bottom.

Use the in-app button

Open Pulse → Settings → Contact support. It opens your Mail app prefilled with the app version, iOS version, and device model — exactly the details needed to reproduce a widget bug. No data is sent until you tap send, and you can edit or remove any of it first.

If a widget is showing --, please leave that detail in — see the FAQ below for what it means.

Frequently asked questions

Where does my heart-rate data go?

Nowhere. Pulse reads heart-rate samples from HealthKit on your device, computes the widget, and that's the end of it. There is no Pulse server. Nothing is uploaded, synced, or shared.

Why is my widget showing --?

That sentinel means HealthKit has no heart-rate samples inside the window the widget is configured for — for example, an "Avg, last hour" widget when the Watch hasn't taken a reading in the past hour. Once a new sample arrives the widget will refresh on its normal schedule.

Does Pulse need an internet connection?

No. Pulse works fully offline. There is no account, no sign-in, and no network code in the app.

Why does Pulse ask for HealthKit permissions?

To read your heart-rate samples and workouts so it can compute the stats and draw the charts. Nothing else is requested, and Pulse never writes back to HealthKit.

Does Pulse work without an Apple Watch?

Yes — the iPhone widgets and Lock Screen widgets work with whatever heart-rate data HealthKit has, including readings from third-party devices. An Apple Watch gives you the richest data (and adds the Watch complications), but it isn't required.

Will there be an Android version?

No. Pulse is built on HealthKit, which is Apple-only.

How do I add a Pulse widget?

Long-press the Home Screen, tap the + in the top corner, search for Pulse, pick the widget, and tap Add Widget. The same flow works on the Lock Screen and Apple Watch face.

Can't use the in-app button?

Email pulse.support [at] bearcave.my and please include your iOS version, device model, and Pulse version — it speeds up debugging considerably.
