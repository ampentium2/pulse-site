Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 May 2026

Pulse collects nothing. Your heart-rate data never leaves your device — there is no Pulse server, no account, and no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. Pulse has no analytics, no telemetry, and no crash-reporting SDK. The app does not create an account, does not identify you, and contains no network code that sends your data anywhere.

HealthKit

Pulse reads your heart-rate samples and workouts from HealthKit so it can compute the stats and draw the widgets and charts. This happens entirely on your device. Your health data is never transmitted off the device, is never written back to HealthKit, and is never shared with any third party.

Support email

If you email us for support, the content of your message and your email address are forwarded to and stored in the developer's personal email inbox for the sole purpose of replying to you. This information is not shared with any third party, and it is deleted on request.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies and uses no tracking of any kind. It stores exactly one item in your browser's localStorage — your theme preference, under the key pulse.theme — so the site remembers whether you chose System, Light, or Dark. That value never leaves your browser.

Analytics

None. There is no analytics or visitor measurement on this site or in the app. If a privacy-respecting page counter is ever added in the future, this section will be updated in the same change that adds it.

Children

Pulse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone — including children.
